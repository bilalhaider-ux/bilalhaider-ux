### 🔬 Applied Engineering & Statistical Moat

- **Mathematical & Inferential Rigor:** Hypothesis testing, parametric/non-parametric distribution modeling, and survival analysis rooted in formal statistical training.
- **Leakage-Free Predictive ML:** GBDT architectures (LightGBM, CatBoost) built with strict Out-of-Time (OOT) splits and asymmetric cost-matrix threshold optimization.
- **Multimodal GenAI & LLM Orchestration:** Document intelligence systems, semantic retrieval (RAG), and agentic workflows via Google Gemini 2.5 Flash and LangChain.
- **Full-Stack Systems:** End-to-end applications designed with Next.js (App Router), React, and FastAPI/Django, backed by PostgreSQL and Supabase.
